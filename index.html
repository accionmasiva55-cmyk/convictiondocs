Product How It Works

The internet talks.
Conviction keeps the receipts.

Crypto Twitter makes thousands of calls a day and remembers none of them. CONVICTION turns every tweet into a tradeable market so anyone can take the other side. Put money behind what you say.

1

See a KOL's tweet (The Call)

Every tweet is a market. It starts with a tweet containing a claim: a price target, a specific date, or a bold prediction. For example, a KOL says "ETH > $8K before 2027".

It stays exactly as it was written — the market settles that exact wording, so an edit or a delete afterwards changes nothing. We immortalize the call.

2

Pick a side: Back it or Fade it

Trade based on your conviction. Anyone can act as a market maker on Crypto Twitter by staking capital on the outcome they believe in:

  • BACK Agree with the call (Green). You think they are right.
  • FADE Disagree with the call (Red). You think they are wrong.
3

The market resolves

When the time comes, the outcome is settled. Those who were right get paid; those who were wrong lose their stake.

Your reputation builds with every trade:

  • Conviction Score: Moves based on your win rate, volume, and consistency.
  • Immutable Receipts: Win and get a "👑 HE CALLED IT" badge. Lose and get a "💀 BAD CALL" badge to share directly on X.
