What Is CONVICTION?

Every tweet is a market. CONVICTION is Crypto Twitter turned into prediction markets — the Terminal for CT, where a KOL’s call becomes a tradeable position and your reputation moves with every trade.

Overview

Crypto Twitter already runs on conviction. A KOL makes the call, the timeline piles on, and the argument plays out in public for days. But talk is free — the loudest take costs exactly the same as the right one, and nobody keeps score.

CONVICTION makes you put money behind what you say. Any tweet worth arguing about becomes a market with explicit resolution criteria: BACK IT. if you think the caller is right, FADE IT. if you think they are wrong, PROVE IT. to turn a tweet into a market yourself.

The internet talks. We keep the receipts. When a market resolves, winners get paid and losers lose the stake — and every position leaves a shareable receipt, HE CALLED IT or BAD CALL, plus a Conviction Score from 0 to 100 that follows your handle everywhere. Your reputation is your portfolio.

Product Core Mechanics

Core Mechanics

1. BACK / FADE

Every tweet from a KOL automatically becomes a two-sided market. There's no "maybe" — you BACK it if you think the tweet will come true, or FADE it if you think it won't. The price on each side reflects the market's implied probability, the same way Polymarket works: if BACK costs $0.70, the market is pricing a 70% chance the event happens.

2. Market lifecycle

Every market goes through the same five stages: the tweet is detected (by a curator or via PROVE IT.), the market opens with an explicit resolution criteria, the trading window opens, the market resolves by verifying the real-world outcome, and winners get paid while every participant's Conviction Score updates.

Phase 1

Tweet detected

By a platform curator or a Level 10+ user via PROVE IT.

Phase 2

Market opens

Explicit resolution criteria and deadlines are set in stone.

Phase 3

Trading window

Users buy shares for BACK or FADE based on their conviction. Market dictates the live probability.

Phase 4

Market resolves

Deadline is reached; real-world outcome is officially verified.

Phase 5

Payout + Score update

Winners receive funds. Everyone's Conviction Score adjusts.

Resolution Criteria

Defined rigidly at market inception by Curators or Level 10+ users. It uses verifiable data sources (e.g., Coingecko for prices, official announcements for events). No room for interpretation.

Deleted Tweets

We keep the receipts. The market settles based on the exact original wording captured at creation. If a KOL deletes or edits their tweet, the market remains active and resolves normally.

Time Window

Typically ranges from 24 hours (for fast-paced CT drama) to 6 months (for macro calls). The exact lock and resolution times are always visible before you place a trade.

3. Receipts

The outcome turns into an immediate social artifact: "HE CALLED IT" if you were right, "BAD CALL" if you weren't. Both are shareable on X in one click — this isn't just cosmetic, it's the product's main organic growth loop: every shared receipt is free marketing with built-in context.

KOL Battles

Two KOLs, opposing calls, one market. This is the most CT-native category — it's built for the rivalry dynamic that already drives engagement on Twitter, now with money behind it.

Example Market Ansem vs ChartWizard — ETH > $8K before 2027?

Price Calls

A specific, falsifiable price prediction from a single KOL. The simplest category to resolve — the outcome is a number crossing a threshold by a deadline.

Example Market ZEC > $1,000? / BTC > $150K?

Market Structure

Macro or structural takes on the crypto market itself, not a single asset's price. These require a clearer resolution window since "outperform" needs a defined timeframe.

Example Market Will ETH outperform BTC (over the next 30 days)?

Meme & Culture

CT drama, callouts, and social dynamics — the category that's unique to CONVICTION and doesn't exist on Polymarket or Kalshi. This is what makes the product feel "social" rather than "financial."

Example Market Will [KOL] get roasted for this take?

AI & Meta

Longer-horizon, speculative calls about the future of AI/crypto convergence. Lower liquidity, higher engagement — good for retention since these markets stay open and talked-about for longer.

Example Market AGI by 2027?
