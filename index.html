Product Core Mechanics

Core Mechanics

1. BACK / FADE

Every tweet from a KOL becomes a two-sided market. There is no “maybe”: you BACK the call if you think it lands, or FADE it if you think it does not. Both sides stay open for as long as the market is live.

Prices are quoted in cents, and every share settles at $1.00 if its side is right and $0.00 if it is wrong. That makes the price the market’s implied probability: BACK trading at 70¢ means the crowd is pricing a 70% chance the call lands. The two sides always add up to $1.00 — if BACK is 70¢, FADE is 30¢.

Your downside is capped at what you paid. Buy 100 BACK shares at 70¢ and you stake $70 to win $100. Take the other side and $30 wins the same $100. The cheaper the side, the bigger the payoff for being right — markets reward clarity, not noise.

Every market goes through the same five stages: the tweet is detected (by a curator or via PROVE IT.), the market opens with an explicit resolution criteria, the trading window opens, the market resolves by verifying the real-world outcome, and winners get paid while every participant's Conviction Score updates.

Resolution Criteria

Defined rigidly at market inception by Curators or Level 10+ users. It uses verifiable data sources (e.g., Coingecko for prices, official announcements for events). No room for interpretation.

Deleted Tweets

We keep the receipts. The market settles based on the exact original wording captured at creation. If a KOL deletes or edits their tweet, the market remains active and resolves normally.

Time Window

Typically ranges from 24 hours (for fast-paced CT drama) to 6 months (for macro calls). The exact lock and resolution times are always visible before you place a trade.

Your first trade on CONVICTION cannot really go wrong. If the call misses, you get the stake back — up to $100.

The hardest trade anyone makes is the first one. Not because it is complicated — you pick BACK or FADE and the order fills — but because putting real money behind a take for the first time feels different from posting one.

So we take that first one off the table. Trade the take you actually believe in, at a size you actually mean, and find out what it feels like to be on the hook.
